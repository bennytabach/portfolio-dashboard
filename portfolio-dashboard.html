Portfolio Commentary — 1 November 2026

Overall assessment. Against the last saved snapshot (April 2026), six of the seven positions are higher: Nasdaq-100 (EQQS) +22%, SMH +20%, COWZ +14%, SPY +11%, IBIT +9%, EM +1%. ITA lags at −8%. October hurt. SPY fell three weeks in a row, SMH dropped 7% on 30 Oct and is ~22% off its high, and IEMG lost ~9% as Korean and Taiwanese chipmakers sold off. Behind the moves: sticky inflation (PCE 3.6%), a hawkish Fed on hold, $100+ oil on Hormuz tensions, and doubts about whether AI capex will pay off.

Concentration risks. The portfolio is largely one AI-semiconductor bet. Nvidia is ~8% of SPY, a top-3 Nasdaq-100 name and ~20% of SMH. TSMC, Samsung and SK Hynix lead the EM fund and also sit in SMH. At the target weights (25/25/15/10/10/10/5), over a third of the money tracks the chip cycle. COWZ is the only real counterweight.

Top 3 conviction calls.

  • COWZ (Buy). 12.3x forward P/E and a 1.9% yield. Its energy, refiner and defense tilt hedges the AI factor.
  • EM / IEMG (Buy). 9.3x forward P/E, about half the US multiple, after October's drop.
  • SMH (Buy, accumulate slowly). 16.5x forward P/E for 40%+ earnings growth. Size it carefully: beta is 1.8.

Forward recommendations.

  • Rebalancing: no sales are needed. Let new money do the rebalancing, which keeps capital-gains tax at zero.
  • Monthly $1,350: SPY $350 · COWZ $300 · EM $250 · SMH $200 · NDX $150 · ITA $50 · IBIT $50. This lightens NDX, the priciest sleeve.

VXUS is cheap, but it overlaps with EM and goes against the no-developed-ex-US preference. XLE is at peak-cycle earnings, and QTUM and BOTZ add more tech exposure.
