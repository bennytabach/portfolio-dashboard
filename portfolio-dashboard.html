Concentration Risks

The portfolio's primary structural risk is Nvidia exposure appearing across three holdings simultaneously: SMH (~15% of fund), EQQS.L/NASDAQ-100 (~7.3% of fund), and SPY (~4.5% of fund). Effectively, ~10-12% of the overall portfolio's performance is tied to a single company's earnings trajectory. A Nvidia earnings miss, export control escalation, or multiple compression would simultaneously hit all three positions. A secondary risk is Taiwan/TSMC concentration: it appears in SMH, EQQS.L, and the EM ETF (תכלית/IEMG proxy), creating compounding geopolitical tail risk around the Taiwan Strait. The ITA + aerospace position (pure US defense policy risk) and IBIT (Bitcoin volatility, regulatory risk) add high-variance tail exposure, though they serve distinct portfolio functions.

1. COWZ (portfolio) — add on dips. At 14x forward P/E with a 2.1% yield, COWZ provides the only true value-factor diversification in an otherwise growth-heavy book.
